# WGMMA v5/v5.1 — cp.async staging + 4-stage pipeline (2026-09-28)

## Results (N=4096, verify PASS all)

| version | design | TFLOPS | node |
|---|---|---:|---|
| v4 | LDG direct load, 2-stage dbuf | 135.82 | h100-0-3 |
| v5 | cp.async 16B, 2-stage | 130.04 | h100-0-22 |
| v5.1 | cp.async 16B, 4-stage | **142.42** | h100-0-1 |

Cross-node numbers (spread ~7%); direction consistent.

## Lessons

1. v5 (2-stage cp.async) ≈ v4: with only 2 stages the copy overlaps just
   the tail of the wgmma — depth, not async-ness alone, is what hides HBM
   latency.
2. v5.1 verify bug: `cp_async_wait<STAGES-1>` over-permits. Groups newer
   than tile i at wait time: only i+1, i+2 (i+3 issues after the wait) —
   correct depth is min(2, tilesLeft), clamped to 0 at the tail.
   Lesson: count groups the SAME way the hardware does (issue order), not
   by stage-array size.
3. cp.async participates in the generic proxy; fence.proxy.async between
   cp.async completion and wgmma issue retained (v4-verified ordering).

## Status vs roofline

AI = 64 FLOP/B -> ~230T HBM cap. v5.1 at 142.42T = 62% of cap.
Remaining gap: 2x __syncthreads per iteration (512 threads), single 16B
cp.async per thread per stage (issue rate), no L2-aware scheduling.
